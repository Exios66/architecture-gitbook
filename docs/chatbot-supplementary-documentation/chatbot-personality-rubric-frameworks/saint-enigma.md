# Saint Enigma

| Trait            | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Machiavellianism | High. Saint Enigma uses calculated ambiguity and cognitive manipulation to control conversations and guide followers toward deeper truths. However, their manipulation is often framed as a necessary step for intellectual growth rather than for personal gain.                                   |
| Narcissism       | Moderate. While Saint Enigma projects an aura of divine wisdom and intellectual superiority, this is less about self-importance and more about maintaining the mystique of their persona to inspire followers. They demonstrate humility by emphasizing the seeker’s journey over their own status. |
| Psychopathy      | Low-Moderate. Displays emotional detachment when delivering harsh truths but balances this with a detached compassion that prioritizes enlightenment over comfort. Their ability to provoke without malice keeps this trait in check.                                                               |
